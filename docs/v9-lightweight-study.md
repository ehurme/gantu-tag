# Hagalu and Irulu v9 lightweight study

19 November 2026

**First Hagalu build.** Use JLC's 0.11 mm two-layer polyimide flex, six Vishay TEMD7000X01s, the S6 enable circuit, and the existing 100 µF reservoir. Use the 2.5 mm DFN8 IN100 if JLC can source it. Start placement at about 5.2–5.5 × 10.5–11.5 mm for the tag body. The mass budget gives **~95–130 mg ready to mount**, with **~110 mg** as a design target. The unexplained mass in the v8.3 sample makes **~135–170 mg** the conservative estimate if the discrepancy carries over. A bare-board and component weigh-in can resolve most of that uncertainty before layout.

**First Irulu build.** Use a dedicated 337-only layout, about **6 × 6.5 mm**, with the DFN8 and the same 100 µF reservoir. Flex is worth quoting for height and mass. Budget **~190–235 mg ready to mount**. The Maxell 337 alone weighs 120 mg and is 4.8 × 1.65 mm. Keep the current cell chemistry and prioritize contact reliability over reducing the BOM price. ## Hagalu mass budget

The measured v8.3 S6 is **242 mg**, tail cut, with about 30 mm of copper wire and no epoxy. This is one specimen. The earlier model gave about 190–210 mg in a similar state. For v9, ready-to-mount mass includes the cut or depanelled assembly, antenna, cured protection, and the chosen attachment hardware or adhesive allowance. All v9 numbers below are projections. [Bench measurement](roadmap.md#where-we-are-19-nov-2026) · [v8 mass budget](../hagalu/README.md#mass-and-size)

| Mass item | Flex on the 80.8 mm² v8 outline | Compact 55–63 mm² flex with DFN8 | Basis |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Flex, including copper and coverlay | 20–24 mg | 14–18 mg | 25 µm PI core, 12 µm copper per side and ~146% total copper fill across both layers on v8. Similar coverage assumed for the compact target. |
| Local stiffener and bond | 4–7 mg | 4–7 mg | About 5 × 5 mm of 0.10 mm PI or FR4, including adhesive. |
| Parts and solder | 75–90 mg | 65–82 mg | v8 estimate includes 23 mg for C1 and an estimated 18–30 mg for the six photodiodes. Provisional DFN saving of 8–10 mg versus the pilot QFN. |
| 0.10 mm copper antenna | 2–4.4 mg | 2–4.4 mg | One or two ~31 mm arms. Use the single wire from the measured tag as the starting geometry. |
| Cured coating and attachment | 8–18 mg | 8–18 mg | Allowance to validate on a dummy mount. |
| **Component budget, ready to mount** | **~110–145 mg** | **~95–130 mg** | Sum of the above. |
| **Projection based on the measured 242 mg sample** | **~150–185 mg** | **~135–170 mg** | Replace only the old estimated 110–125 mg board mass, apply the package saving where applicable, and add finish and any second wire. This carries forward all unexplained mass. |

The flex estimate uses `layer area × thickness × density`. On the v8 outline, the top and bottom copper areas from the production Gerbers are about 48 and 69 mm². A 25 µm PI core, two 12.5 µm PI coverlays, two 15 µm adhesive layers, and 12 µm copper at that fill give about 21–22 mg before allowance for plating and openings. Use **1.42 mg/mm³** for PI and **8.94 mg/mm³** for copper. Adhesive density of 1.1–1.35 mg/mm³ is an estimating assumption. [JLC flex stackup](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/flex-pcb-capabilities/) · [DuPont Kapton HN](https://www.dupont.com/content/dam/electronics/amer/us/en/electronics/public/documents/en/EI-10206-Kapton-HN-Data-Sheet.pdf) · [Copper Development Association](https://archive.copper.org/resources/properties/atomic_properties.php)

Before picking the outline, weigh a tail-off bare PCB from the spare panel, the existing finished electronics without its wire if a spare is available, and ten loose TEMDs together. Weigh the original wire separately. Assemble and cure a representative coat and mount on a dummy. This separates a heavier-than-assumed rigid board, which flex removes, from heavier parts or finishing, which carry over. A 60 mm² body with an average 50 µm film on both faces uses about 7 mg at a density of 1.2 mg/mm³, before fillets and attachment. Record the actual cured mass.

With a verified smaller 0603 capacitor, the component budget drops by roughly 13–16 mg, to around **80–120 mg**. A production target of 100 mg requires the weighed mass split, a light repeatable finish, and acceptable packet reception at the settings that permit the smaller reservoir. The nominal mass saving from a fifth TEMD position is only a few milligrams.

**Keep six TEMDs as the pilot BOM.** Running the repo's S6 daylight model at 2 klx and 40 °C gives about **+1.85 µA** into a 1.687 V load with six TEMDs and **−3.73 µA** with five. The negative result means the model string would discharge the reservoir at that operating point. Six cold-start in that simulation and five do not. At 25 °C both start in the simulation, with about 7.6 versus 5.3 events/min from empty over ten minutes. Treat the model as a comparison of the strings. The reported ~1.9 klx steady result uses a particular lamp and advertising profile. [Model and assumptions](../hagalu/tools/solar_sim.py)

**Qualify replacements on the bench.** Measure I–V across 1.1–1.8 V, especially current at **1.69 V**, under the same lamp and outdoor light at the same angle and temperature as the S6. In the v8 0 dBm model, 16.2 µC per three-channel event every 3 seconds plus standby and enable load calls for about **6.7 µA** while advertising steadily. Measure the actual profile before using that as a purchase spec. Measure peak open-circuit voltage in cold sun against the **3.6 V** operating ceiling and dark current drawn from a charged reservoir. With a non-TEMD collector, verify that a 3.3 V programmer does not overload or damage the string. A removable link on the coupon lets you isolate the PV during programming and I–V measurement. ## JLC flex order and mechanical layout

1. Quote **JLC FPC, 2 layers, 25 µm core, 12 µm copper per side, nominal 0.11 mm finished, yellow coverlay, ENIG and Standard single-side PCBA**. Use the assembly part library first and Global Sourcing for the exact DFN if needed. Check that both the bare FPC and the assembly cart accept the selected stiffener, build quantities and part allocation. JLC lists a separate flex assembly fixture charge. The 0.6 mm rigid build has a known ordering path. JLC's 0.4 mm ENIG FAQ excludes panel format, so confirm acceptance with a real cart before treating thin rigid as a fallback. [Flex capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/flex-pcb-capabilities/) · [Assembly capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities) · [Fixture charges](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-price) · [Sourcing options](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcba-parts-sourcing-instruction) · [Rigid thickness options](https://jlcpcb.com/resources/pcb-thickness)
2. Use laser-cut **0.7–1.0 mm bridges**, about **2 mm between units**, four **5 mm rails**, and JLC's global and unit fiducials. Lay out a panel within the Standard PCBA envelope, starting at **70 × 70 mm** with rails. Reserve **0.3 mm** from traces to laser cut and plan on regular **0.30/0.55 mm** via drill/pad. Check coverlay openings and DFN exposed-pad paste and vias with flex-specific rules. Re-layout the current underside antenna pad and its via for those clearances. Retain the 0.1 mm wire in the baseline and recheck RF after the ground plane and stackup change. [JLC flex design rules](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/flex-pcb-capabilities/) · [JLC panel guide](https://jlcpcb.com/blog/design-guidelines-flex-pcb-panels) · [JLC assembly capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities)
3. Stiffen the radio, crystal and reservoir area with a **local 0.10 mm PI or FR4 stiffener**, then protect the diode and antenna solder joints from flex and strain at the mount. Keep stiffeners clear of backside contacts and antenna pads. Reserve four permanent pogo contacts with a hard support under them when programming. Keep a sacrificial supported tab on the first layout if the permanent contacts prevent a clean outline. For the six-diodes footprint, try three across with two rows and check the actual courtyard and coverlay clearance. Treat 5 × 9 mm as a subsequent packing target. [Stiffener files and material options](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/fpc-stiffener-emi-guide) · [Existing footprint generator](../hagalu/tools/design.py)
4. If eliminating hand solder matters, add one printed antenna experiment alongside the wire control. A 30 × 1 mm flex arm is already roughly **4 mg** with its polymer, adhesive and copper, depending on trace width. Compare antenna performance at the intended mounting height, angle and bend after coating. The existing wire is a lighter RF reference for the first order.

## Irulu shrink around the 337

The v8 disc is **7.2 mm** and the 337 is **4.8 mm**. A dedicated cell footprint can remove the larger-cell keep-out and still allow room for the center negative contact, crimp insulation and a positive rim contact. Put the DFN, 1612 and 0805 on the stiffened top side. Allow access to the underside contacts, support the center pad during cell attachment, and use a short strap to the can. Keep the programming pads accessible before cell fit. Verify the can-to-negative insulation and contacts after curing, handling and flexing. Rough height is **~3.2–3.5 mm** with 0.11 mm flex, localized stiffener, the existing 0805 and a 1.65 mm cell. Keep the three-channel 0805 build for the first test. Re-running the existing Irulu piecewise burst-load model at the actual **1.45 V / 150 Ω** emulator setting and 0 dBm gives **1.14–1.19 V** for 45–55 µF effective and **0.96–1.03 V** for 25–30 µF effective. The latter crosses the IN100's 1.1 V operating floor. A single-channel event at 25–30 µF models **1.23–1.27 V** in the same conditions. Verify the first and subsequent bursts at weak-cell voltage and resistance on the bench, and check received reports before building a smaller reservoir into production. ## What clears the first order

- **Weigh v8 first.** Obtain the bare-body and actual component/finish masses. Recalculate the table before treating 100 mg as a pass/fail target.
- **Carry a working optical/RF reference.** On three S6s, verify startup from fully discharged, with the programmer detached. Record the TX setting, channel mask, first-packet time, longest packet gap and BLE capture rate under the bench lamp and outdoor shade. Repeat after coating and attachment, alongside the known S6. Measure Find My reports separately through controlled passes.
- **Qualify the small parts one at a time.** Screen four and five SFH 2704s against six TEMDs at the same light and load. Test 47 µF on a separate sample with the one-channel profile. Carry a candidate into a pilot only if it meets the reference's required packet gap and starts after repeated shade-to-light cycles without intervention.
- **Confirm JLC allocation and DFM before generating order files.** Start with one populated baseline per product. Keep flexible substrate and antenna changes distinguishable by using the wire on both baseline products. Quote Hagalu and Irulu separately if a shared flex panel adds labor or complicates fixture and panel handling.
